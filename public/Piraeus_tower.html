Piraeus Tower Ligihting System development

Project description

This is a media facade lighting project located in front of Piraeus port.

It consists of 4683 linear luminaires, installed into vertical fins, in all sides of the building. Each one can reproduce 100mm pixels of RGBW colors each. All lighting information is transmitted over RS-485 lines with DMX512 signal. The background of this is an Ethernet network based lighting system with a lighting server and a lot of peripheral signal converter units with multiple outputs.

The final lighting effect is really impressive, as the building can be transformed into a giant screen, showing videos, images and animations. The project was a great experience for me as I had the opportunity to work on a large scale media facade and contribute to its design and implementation through lighting technology.

You can watch a video of the lighting system in action, during the christmass lighting experimenting and programming caputured by local people arround the building. The video showcases the transformation of the Piraeus Tower into a giant screen, displaying festive animations and colors.
