Acropolis of Athens Ligihting System development

Project description

In this project, I developed a lighting system for the Acropolis of Athens. The system is designed to provide optimal lighting for the monument while minimizing energy consumption. The system uses LED lights and is controlled by a central lighting server that adjusts the lighting based on the time of day and weather conditions.

There are two parts of lighting for this project. The one is the lighting of the monumemts on the "Sacred Rock", where Parthenon, Erechthio and Propylaia munuments are placed on. These monument's lighting is controlled through DMX signal. The other part of it is the perimetrical lighting which includes the surounding walls of the "Secred Rock" and the monuments on its perimeter. These lights are controlled by ON/OFF commands through power relays and are equiped with dim-on-board control.

Photos and videos are copied from Onassis.org website, which is the company I worked for during this project. The project was a great experience for me as I had the opportunity to work on a historical monument and contribute to its preservation and enhancement through lighting design.
